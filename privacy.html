Privacy policy

We collect as little personal data as possible. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Geo Servicos Administrativo LTDA, operating as GeoAnalytics Lab, CNPJ 60.674.323/0001-92, Rua Barra do Ariranha, 64, Chácara Santo Antônio (Zona Leste), 03408-025 São Paulo – SP, Brazil. We operate in Brazil and have a representation in Barcelona, Spain.

For anything related to privacy, write to contact@geoanalyticslab.com.

2. What we collect and why

Free EUDR forest check

Your plot files are never uploaded. The forest check reads and analyses your GeoJSON, Shapefile, KML or GeoPackage entirely inside your browser. Your plot locations and attributes do not reach us or anyone else. To do the analysis, your browser downloads small pieces of the public forest map and satellite imagery for the area of your plots (see section 4).

If you choose to download the results, we ask you to register. We then receive:

  • your name, organization and email address;
  • a summary of your results: only the percentage of plots with forest and without forest;
  • whether you ticked the box to receive updates;
  • the date and time, the page language and the country your connection comes from (derived from your IP address, which we do not store).

We use this to know who uses the tool, to follow up on your request if useful (for example if many of your plots contain forest), and to improve the service. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box in the registration panel.

Updates: only if you tick the optional box do we send you occasional news about our services (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)). Every message includes a way to unsubscribe.

To save you typing, your browser remembers your name, organization and email for the next download (local storage on your device; we cannot read it). You can delete it by clearing your browser's site data.

When you contact us

If you email us, message us on WhatsApp or LinkedIn, we use your details and message to reply and, if we work together, to prepare and carry out the project. Legal basis: steps at your request before a contract and the contract itself (Art. 6(1)(b)), or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Visiting the website

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Like any website, our hosting and network providers process your IP address and technical data (browser, time of the request) to deliver the pages and protect them against abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3. How long we keep it

  • Forest check registrations: up to 24 months after your last contact with us, or until you withdraw your consent.
  • Updates: until you unsubscribe.
  • Enquiries and client correspondence: as long as needed to handle them, and as long as tax and commercial law require for clients (usually up to 6 years in Spain).

5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect what we did before. Just write to contact@geoanalyticslab.com; we reply within one month.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to a data protection authority. In the EU, that is the authority of your country; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

If you are in Brazil

Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, LGPD, Lei 13.709/2018) also applies. Under it you can ask us to confirm whether we process your data and to access, correct, anonymise, block, delete or port it, to know with whom we share it, and to withdraw your consent (Art. 18). The contact above also handles LGPD requests. You can complain to the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

6. Other points

  • We do not make automated decisions about you, and we do not build profiles.
  • Our services are aimed at businesses and professionals, not at children.
