Good to know

A first screening, not a verdict.

What is checked. Forest presence in 2020 in the JRC Global Forest Cover 2020 map, version 4 (10 m resolution). A map pixel counts when its centre lies inside the plot. Points are checked at their exact location.

What it means. Forest in a plot in 2020 does not by itself mean non-compliance, and no forest in the map does not prove compliance. The JRC map is not legally binding and has known errors, for example with agroforestry, plantations and tree crops. Plots flagged here deserve a closer look with higher-resolution imagery and time series.

Coverage. The tool covers the regions EUDR commodities mainly come from. Not included: everything north of 60° N (northern Canada, Alaska, Greenland, northern Scandinavia and Russia), and Europe and Asia between 50° and 60° N. Plots there are marked "Outside coverage".

Privacy. Your files are read and analysed by your own browser. Only small pieces of the public forest map are downloaded; your plot geometries are not sent anywhere.

Forest map: European Commission, Joint Research Centre (JRC), Global Forest Cover 2020 (GFC2020), version 4, EU Observatory on deforestation and forest degradation. Satellite imagery: Mapbox, Maxar; Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX (Copernicus Sentinel data).
